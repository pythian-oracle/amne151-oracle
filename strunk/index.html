II. Elementary Rules of Usage

  1. 1. Form the possessive singular of nouns with 's.
  2. 2. In a series of three or more terms with a single conjunction, use a comma after each term except the last.
  3. 3. Enclose parenthetic expressions between commas.
  4. 4. Place a comma before and or but introducing an independent clause.
  5. 5. Do not join independent clauses by a comma.
  6. 6. Do not break sentences in two.
  7. 7. A participial phrase at the beginning of a sentence must refer to the grammatical subject.
  8. 8. Divide words at line-ends, in accordance with their formation and pronunciation.

III. Elementary Principles of Composition

  1. 9. Make the paragraph the unit of composition: one paragraph to each topic.
  2. 10. As a rule, begin each paragraph with a topic sentence; end it in conformity with the beginning.
  3. 11. Use the active voice.
  4. 12. Put statements in positive form.
  5. 13. Omit needless words.
  6. 14. Avoid a succession of loose sentences.
  7. 15. Express co-ordinate ideas in similar form.
  8. 16. Keep related words together.
  9. 17. In summaries, keep to one tense.
  10. 18. Place the emphatic words of a sentence at the end.
